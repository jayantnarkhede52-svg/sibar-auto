About Sibar

Decades of Foundry Excellence

Pioneering the science of precision aluminium casting and CNC machining since 1983.

Our Engineering Legacy

SIBAR Auto Parts Limited is a publicly traded enterprise (BSE: 520141) built on uncompromising quality and technological forward-thinking. From our 15,000 sq. meter facility in Tirupati, India, we have been delivering highly engineered aluminium castings and precision-machined components to global automotive partners for over 40 years.

Core Strengths

  • End-to-end manufacturing: Casting, Machining, and Surface Treatment under one roof.
  • Advanced Metallurgy R&D lab with Spectrometers and Real-time X-ray.
  • Zero-defect mindset embedded through our TS 16949 and ISO 9001 workflows.

Corporate Governance

As a listed company, we operate with maximum transparency, financial stability, and environmental responsibility, complying with the latest BSE regulations and ESG frameworks.
